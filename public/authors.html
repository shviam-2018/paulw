Services for Authors




When I first dipped my toes into the world of publishing, it felt like navigating a labyrinth—full of information, misinformation, and countless people offering services at steep prices. I've known authors who have spent tens of thousands just to get their books published, often falling prey to those looking to take advantage.

To help authors avoid these pitfalls, I've guided several newcomers through the publishing process.
Whether you're aiming for traditional or self-publishing, I offer a no-obligation initial chat to hear about your journey so far and provide tailored advice on how to move forward.

I also provide a range of services to assist you in your publishing journey, including:

  • line, copy, and proof editing
  • Formatting and production of digital files ready for print
  • Guidance through the publishing process so you can learn the ropes yourself
  • Creation of book trailers and voiceovers

If you'd like to chat or are interested in any of these services, feel free to get in touch.
